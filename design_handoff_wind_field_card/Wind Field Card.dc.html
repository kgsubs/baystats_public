Concept 1a resolved to production: light and dark themes matched to the live site, plus the four states the data can actually produce. Everything below is measured — see design_handoff_wind_field_card/README.md for tokens, the Open-Meteo contract, and acceptance criteria.

✓ LEE SIDE IS SHELTERED
OUTSIDE THE BAY14 KT
IN THE ANCHORAGE6 KT
Drops between WINDS 9 KT ENE and the phone row. Arrows point the way the wind blows (ENE → WSW); the shaded band is the lee, the two arrows inside it are drawn at 45% opacity and shorter. Arrows are plain — shaft and head only, no barbs or flags; speed is encoded by shaft length alone. Motion is a seamless one-way conveyor: the arrow field is drawn twice, one pitch apart along the wind vector, and the pair translates exactly one pitch per cycle — it never reverses, and frame 0 and frame 1 are identical, so the loop is invisible.

Above 20 kt in the anchorage the green chip is withheld — it becomes a neutral row, arrows go full-white, longer, and travel more than twice as fast, and the lee band disappears because there isn't one. The palette never introduces amber or red; urgency is carried by value and motion.
